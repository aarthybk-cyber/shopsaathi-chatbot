    How Saathi answers

    Saathi never makes up an answer. Each reply follows the same three steps.

    1. Finds the closest FAQ

      Your question is compared with all 50 FAQs, and the best match wins.

    2. Checks the match

      If nothing is close enough, Saathi says so instead of guessing.

    3. Replies or hands over

      A close match is reworded in a friendly voice. Otherwise you can ask for a human agent.
